# Integrated reading task, not a five-volume prerequisite

`view: integrated` organizes selected questions, exact answers, mechanisms and boundaries as one independently readable account. It is available for all three profiles. It does not require foundations/results/methods/boundaries PDFs first.

The supplied compact dHYM sample is a bounded style reference, not an authoritative source for new mathematics or a fixed length/template. Historical samples remain frozen reproduction inputs. For a new topic build and review its own knowledge and output, rather than replay a standard answer into a template. [Legacy Part V reference](legacy/part-v-benchmark.md).
