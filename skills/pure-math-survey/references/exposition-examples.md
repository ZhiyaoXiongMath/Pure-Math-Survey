# Exposition examples

The [balanced dHYM manuscript](../assets/reference-samples/dhym-balanced/README.md), supplied by the user after multiple rounds of polishing, is the principal finished writing and layout reference. Read the actual passage and page when borrowing its treatment of principal answers, necessary proof steps, local definitions or equality boundaries. Its six-page length, outline and result counts do not constrain a new topic. Its mathematical claims still require primary-source checking before reuse.

The [Schur-complement example](https://github.com/ZhiyaoXiongMath/Pure-Math-Survey/tree/main/examples/schur-complement) is a separate workflow demonstration with knowledge, source-reading notes, snapshots, plans and two outputs. The two examples serve distinct writing and workflow roles.

The assets/exposition-examples files remain elementary TeX fixtures for readable formulas, labels and statement/proof shapes. They are not evidence of research-survey reliability. Build checks use copies of the balanced source with supplied and current styles; neither a successful build nor an old review upgrades new mathematical knowledge.

## New-policy positive and adversarial cases

New vNext benchmark sources and findings are maintained under examples/vnext in the
repository; their local full proofs concern known mathematics, never Paper originality.
The installed templates and references expose the same mode responsibilities; benchmark
acceptance requires actual writing, compilation and reading, not just available filenames.

Minimal positive: completion of the square gives the Schur complement, and the third
section uses invertibility of the coordinate change for both directions. Negative: the
correct three headings contain an unused tool list and only “standard arguments.”
Thematic positive: compare square completion and congruence under the same A>0 assumption.
Negative: call criteria equivalent after dropping positivity on one side. A single real
route is permitted. Lecture positive: expand the substitution and justify why nonzero
vectors stay nonzero. Negative: promise the entire argument but omit necessity.
Paper negative: label that classical argument a new theorem without authorized original
work and prior-art review. Such an article cannot validate formal research quality.

Specialist positive: identify the uniform Hölder bound, exact strong convergence topology,
operator continuity and a uniform cone-interior margin. Negative: “take a weak limit”
without a topology that passes the nonlinear operator or a margin that preserves admissibility.
A reviewer must record the actual defect and location. These are mathematical reading
cases, not a claim that static code automatically detects such errors.
