# Exposition examples

The assets/exposition-examples files are elementary TeX style fixtures. They are not mathematical source-reading or new-profile acceptance. Use them only to verify readable formulas, labels and statement/proof shapes. Current topic examples in the repository examples/ contain their own knowledge, plans, snapshots and evidence; they are not embedded answers in the installed skill.

The compact/v6 dHYM PDFs in assets/reference-samples are historical style/reproduction materials. Their PASS records, dates and literature assertions never upgrade a new node or output. [Historical example notes](legacy/exposition-examples.md).
