# Exposition examples

The [balanced dHYM manuscript](../assets/reference-samples/dhym-balanced/README.md), supplied by the user after multiple rounds of polishing, is the principal finished writing and layout reference. Read the actual passage and page when borrowing its treatment of principal answers, necessary proof steps, local definitions or equality boundaries. Its six-page length, outline and result counts do not constrain a new topic. Its mathematical claims still require primary-source checking before reuse.

The [Schur-complement example](https://github.com/ZhiyaoXiongMath/Pure-Math-Survey/releases/tag/v2.0.3) is a separate workflow demonstration with knowledge, source-reading notes, snapshots, plans and two outputs. The two examples serve distinct writing and workflow roles.

The assets/exposition-examples files remain elementary TeX fixtures for readable formulas, labels and statement/proof shapes. They are not evidence of research-survey reliability. Build checks use copies of the balanced source with supplied and current styles; neither a successful build nor an old review upgrades new mathematical knowledge. The compact/v6 dHYM files are available in historical releases, not the current installation.
