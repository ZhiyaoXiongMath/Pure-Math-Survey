# Check mathematical actions, not old sample answers

Use current templates and [mode contracts](editions.md), plus actual primary sources and declared reader capabilities. Historical generated manuscripts and old bundled knowledge are not current recommended writing inputs or acceptance evidence. New public exemplars, when independently accepted, must identify source/snapshot/profile/view and actual review limitations; do not infer endorsement from a directory name.

For any decisive connection ask: what object/input is available, what operation is performed, what equality/estimate/construction is justified, which condition is indispensable, and who consumes the output? A two-way claim needs both implications; an invertible transformation must establish invertibility under the actual hypotheses. A singular or endpoint case must be handled or excluded explicitly. Correct section headings cannot turn an unused tool list and “standard arguments” into a proof.

Minimal/r4 places the full claims in section one, callable ingredients in section two and decisive connections in section three. Lecture teaches promised steps reproducibly; Thematic compares genuine routes on matching assumptions; Paper explains and proves authorized contributions with honest known/draft status. A concise passage from another mode does not by itself discharge these duties.

The installed assets/exposition-examples snippets are neutral typography/build inputs, not sources or mathematical acceptance records. Quality is checked on the actual source-grounded target, reader, fast/slow passages, TeX and current PDF. Keep attempted reconstructions, concrete calculations, first failures, repairs and remaining scope. No page count, formula quota, accepted flag or synthetic fixture proves understanding.
