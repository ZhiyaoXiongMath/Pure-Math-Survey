# When does a bounded sequence pass a nonlinear equation?

## Target and route
Fix the compact background and 0<beta<alpha<1 before the sequence. A uniform C^{2,alpha} bound supplies equicontinuity of derivatives, then diagonal extraction and interpolation give strong C^{2,beta} convergence. A separate uniform positive margin keeps the limit inside the equation's domain. Joint continuity then passes the equation. See [extraction](N-H-compact.md#derivative-extraction), [interpolation](N-H-compact.md#interpolation), [PDE passage](N-H-main.md#domain-before-equation) and [failure models](N-H-boundary.md#failure-models). No unspecified PDE estimate is proved.
