# Three local mechanisms and their precise limits

These are three independent teaching questions, with separate articles rather than a claimed common mathematical theorem. Every result is classical or an elementary conditional deduction. No original research is supplied or authorized.

## Schur question
Given fixed real symmetric blocks A,C and a rectangular block B, decide strict positivity of their block matrix. Assume A is positive definite for the criterion and minimization. Separately identify what survives under mere invertibility. The reader can expand quadratic forms and invert a triangular change of variables; the Schur complement is constructed here. Singular-block extensions are outside scope.

## Compactness question
Fix a compact smooth manifold without boundary, a smooth metric, a finite atlas and subordinate cutoffs. Given solutions with a uniform C^{2,alpha} estimate, determine the weaker topology in which a subsequence converges and the extra assumptions passing an equation and strict admissibility. All constants are independent of the sequence index; none is claimed to follow from an unspecified PDE. The reader knows multivariable derivatives, integration along line segments, compactness of bounded real sequences, and smooth partitions of unity. Diagonal extraction, Holder interpolation, chart localization and positivity preservation are established. No arbitrary nonlinear weak-limit theorem, a priori estimate, boundary regularity or smoothness bootstrap is asserted.

## Eigenvalue question
On a closed connected smooth Riemannian m-manifold, m>=2, with Ric >= (m-1)g, prove that the first positive eigenvalue of Delta=-div grad is at least m. The reader can use gradient, covariant Hessian and the divergence theorem. The scalar Bochner identity and compact elliptic spectral existence are precise imports; the integral identity, trace estimate, eigenfunction substitution and division are worked out. No equality rigidity is claimed.
