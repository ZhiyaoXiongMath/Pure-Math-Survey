# Local mechanisms, with their inputs visible

This knowledge project contains three independent routes. They share a teaching concern, not a claimed mathematical equivalence: each conclusion becomes available only after an explicit conversion, and its hypotheses must survive that conversion. The separate articles preserve the different reader prerequisites.

## Eliminating a coupled variable
The Schur question starts from the mixed term 2x^TBy. [Completing the square](nodes/N-S-square.md#derivation) constructs the residual form; [both positivity directions](nodes/N-S-main.md#both-directions) use an invertible change and a specially chosen test vector. Strict A>0 additionally produces a unique minimum. [The negative scalar block](nodes/N-S-boundary.md#models-and-failure) shows exactly why invertibility alone cannot do so.

## Closing a sequence of equations
A uniform estimate is an input, not a slogan. With [fixed localized norms](nodes/N-H-norm.md#why-localization-is-fixed), [diagonal extraction](nodes/N-H-compact.md#derivative-extraction) gives uniform derivative convergence and [interpolation](nodes/N-H-compact.md#interpolation) makes the topology C^{2,beta}. The [positive margin and equation passage](nodes/N-H-main.md#domain-before-equation) are separate steps. The [failure models](nodes/N-H-boundary.md#failure-models) distinguish a lost strict inequality, an unavailable endpoint exponent, weak nonlinear limits and local rather than global convergence.

## From curvature to an eigenvalue
The [Bochner and spectral imports](nodes/N-L-inputs.md#canonical-statement) make the relevant identities callable. [Integration and the Hessian trace](nodes/N-L-connection.md#integration-and-trace) convert local curvature into an inequality for a single eigenfunction. The [final positive-factor division](nodes/N-L-main.md#closing-the-estimate) proves the normalized bound. [Dimension one and boundary terms](nodes/N-L-boundary.md#dimension-and-discarded-information) identify where this proof stops.
