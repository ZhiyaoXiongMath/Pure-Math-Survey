# When is a coupled quadratic form positive?

## Target and route
For fixed symmetric A,C and rectangular B, determine strict positivity of the block matrix when A>0. The cross term suggests solving Az=Ax+By. The [square mechanism](N-S-square.md#derivation) removes the coupling, and [the criterion](N-S-main.md#both-directions) tests the remaining form. The [boundary](N-S-boundary.md#models-and-failure) distinguishes invertibility from positivity.
