# How does positive Ricci curvature force a spectral gap?

## Target and route
For Delta=-div grad on a closed connected m-manifold, Ric>=(m-1)g and m>=2 should imply lambda_1>=m. The [precise imports](N-L-inputs.md#canonical-statement) supply a smooth eigenfunction and Bochner. [Integration and trace](N-L-connection.md#integration-and-trace) turn the local curvature identity into a scalar inequality. The [final division](N-L-main.md#closing-the-estimate) records every positive factor. Equality rigidity is excluded.
