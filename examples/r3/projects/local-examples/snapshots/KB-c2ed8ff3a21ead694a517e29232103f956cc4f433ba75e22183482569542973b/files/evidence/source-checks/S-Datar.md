# Current source reading: Datar
Read Lecture 29, Sections 29.1-29.2, printed pp.217-219 (PDF pp.225-227). The actual page images were opened. Proposition 29.1.1 states the scalar Bochner identity for L=div grad. Theorem 29.2.1 supplies smooth eigenfunctions on a compact boundaryless manifold; Theorem 29.2.2 and Lemma 29.2.3 give the eigenvalue argument and Hessian trace inequality. Only the lower bound is used; the equality/rigidity discussion is excluded.

Our Delta is -L. Thus the imported formula becomes -Delta(|grad f|^2)/2=|Hess f|^2-<grad Delta f,grad f>+Ric(grad f,grad f). The integrated identity follows by the divergence theorem. The source prints an extra factor lambda_1 multiplying (n-1-lambda_1)|grad u|^2 in two lines on p.219, but its next displayed line and final integral use the correct coefficient without this factor. Direct substitution L u=-lambda_1 u shows why that factor must be removed. We do not import those erroneous lines. The Hessian coordinate display also prints a first-derivative numerator over two differentials; we use the covariant Hessian and its trace identity, not that typographical expression.

The new proof explicitly assumes m>=2, connectedness, no boundary, Ric>=(m-1)g, and the nonnegative Laplacian. Source theorem wording is read with the section's no-boundary convention; we do not infer a boundary theorem. Bochner's local proof is not supplied in the source's empty proof paragraph, so it is honestly imported rather than claimed proved here. The spectral theorem is also imported.
