# What positivity supplies

## Models and failure
For A=2,B=1,C=1, S=1/2 and Q=2(x+y/2)^2+y^2/2. The minimum at x=-y/2 is y^2/2.

For A=-1,B=0,C=1, the same algebra has S=1 but M=diag(-1,1) is indefinite, and for fixed y, Q=-x^2+y^2 is unbounded below. Invertibility preserves the identity, not minimization or positivity. For A=0 the inverse formula is undefined. This failure of the formula is not a claim that no generalized singular-block theorem exists.
