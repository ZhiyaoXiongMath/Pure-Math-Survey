# Further classical branches and their exact evidence gaps

## Explanation and evidence

This node is unassessed as coverage, not mathematically open.

| Branch | Mathematical input beyond this core | Current evidence and next step |
|---|---|---|
| Heintze–Karcher tube/normal-exponential comparison | An isometrically immersed compact submanifold, its second fundamental form/mean curvature, normal directions, focal and cut bounds | Original 1978 introduction and §2 through Theorem 2.2 (pp.451–454) read. No complete canonical formula imported; the Jacobi determinant proof and all dimension/codimension hypotheses must be checked next. |
| Cheng Dirichlet eigenvalue comparison | First Dirichlet eigenvalue, variational/PDE interface, geodesic-ball boundary and radius conditions | 1975 publisher metadata verified; original full text blocked. Do not infer this statement from the checked maximal-diameter theorem. |
| Lichnerowicz–Obata and spectral gap rigidity | Closed manifolds, eigenfunction normalization and Bochner identity | Named candidate; original statements and equality proof not yet read. |
| Lévy–Gromov isoperimetry | Relative volume normalization, perimeter and minimizers | Named central branch; not implied by the present ball-volume estimates without additional arguments. |
| Rauch–Berger focal comparison | Nonzero initial Jacobi data determined by a submanifold shape operator | Eschenburg Rauch II seen, but full focal/range hypotheses not promoted to a canonical result. |
| Critical-point topology, soul and sphere theorems | Distance critical points, injectivity and topology arguments | Eschenburg contents and some introductory passages inspected, not a theorem-level evidence check. |
| Almost rigidity, convergence and noncollapse | Quantitative deficits, volume scales, topology of convergence | Separate follow-up investigation; no frontier openness claim. |

Weighted/Bakry–Émery, integral curvature, Alexandrov/CAT/RCD, Lorentzian and Finsler extensions remain outside the first checked smooth pointwise-curvature core. The whole project title is not reduced to the first completed branch. Publication plans, if later requested, must select a subset and account for these gaps.
