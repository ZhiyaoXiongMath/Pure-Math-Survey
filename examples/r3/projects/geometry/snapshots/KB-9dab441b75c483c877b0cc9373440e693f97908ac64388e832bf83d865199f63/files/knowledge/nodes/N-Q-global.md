# Which global topology and rigidity follow from comparison?

## Explanation and evidence

For every connected complete smooth manifold satisfying the indicated curvature bound, ask whether the local comparison controls diameter, the exponential map, or an isometric product. Separate Ric >= (n-1)k g with one constant k>0 from merely positive Ricci at each point; distinguish a line from an arbitrary complete geodesic; and distinguish local model equality from global rigidity.

The checked statements are [[N-R-myers]], [[N-R-hadamard]], [[N-R-cheng]] and [[N-R-splitting]]. Theorems keep their own assumptions: simple connectivity is required for the diffeomorphism conclusion of Cartan–Hadamard, while a line is required for splitting.
