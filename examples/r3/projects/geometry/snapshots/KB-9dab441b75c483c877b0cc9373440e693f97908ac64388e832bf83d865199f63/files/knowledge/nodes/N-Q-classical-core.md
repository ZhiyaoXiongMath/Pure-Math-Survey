# The classical point-centered comparison chain

## Explanation and evidence

For smooth connected Riemannian n-manifolds without boundary, n>=2, how do pointwise sectional or Ricci bounds compare radial Jacobi lengths, the regular distance Hessian or Laplacian, concentric-ball volume ratios, diameter, the exponential covering, and nondegenerate minimizing triangles of perimeter strictly below 2D_k? Fix each metric and comparison constant; state completeness where used. Distinguish individual directions from their trace, cut time from conjugate time, and local differential inequalities from global metric conclusions.

The seven principal interfaces are [[N-R-rauch]], [[N-R-hessian]], [[N-R-laplacian]], [[N-R-bishop-gromov]], [[N-R-myers]], [[N-R-hadamard]], and [[N-R-toponogov]]. This is a focused reading task inside the broader project, not a claim that comparison geometry has only seven theorems. It asks for decisive connecting mechanisms with precisely imported prerequisites, not complete proofs of all geometric foundations or triangle gluing.

The reader has studied smooth manifolds, linear algebra, multivariable calculus and elementary ODEs; no previous comparison geometry or connection calculus is presumed. [[N-D-riemannian]] supplies the metric/connection bridge and [[N-M-geometric-inputs]] identifies the imported global interfaces. General equality classifications, rigidity/splitting, degenerate triangles and the spherical perimeter endpoint, focal/tube, spectral, isoperimetric and synthetic extensions remain outside this focused question. Broader question records retain their pending gaps.

## Reading tasks for this question

The [reader entrance](../overview.md#reader-entrance-and-available-operations) supplies the operations needed before Jacobi fields. The [Ricci route](../overview.md#ricci-lower-bounds-to-ball-volume) asks the reader to reconstruct the path from one radial trace inequality to a ball-volume ratio, and to reproduce the actual density and averaging calculations. The [sectional route](../overview.md#sectional-bounds-to-jacobi-length-and-hessian) separates those scalar conclusions from directional ones and gives two concrete ways to obtain the lower-sectional Hessian bound.

The three global routes ask for different missing steps: [Myers](../overview.md#myers-from-endpoint-fields-to-global-finiteness) needs a negative fixed-end index calculation and completeness; [Hadamard](../overview.md#hadamard-from-local-invertibility-to-a-global-cover) needs a complete covering after local nonsingularity; [Toponogov](../overview.md#toponogov-from-local-lengths-to-global-triangles) needs global control of triangles after a local length comparison.

A successful use keeps the model-positive interval, actual conjugate interval, uncut distance domain and strict triangle perimeter distinct. Changing sectional to Ricci curvature, reversing a curvature inequality, omitting cut truncation, dropping completeness or removing simple connectivity must be tested at the first affected operation, not by reversing or deleting words in the final theorem.
