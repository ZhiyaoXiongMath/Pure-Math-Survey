Normalization: R(X,Y)Z=∇X∇YZ−∇Y∇XZ−∇[X,Y]Z; radial curvature R(·,V)V; Ric is unnormalized; a=tr A/(n−1); Δ=div grad. The source's letter A is the shape operator, whereas this KB calls it S and reserves A for the Jacobi tensor. Busemann functions in this source use lim(d−t), the negative of the KB/EH1984 convention.
