# How does a sectional lower bound control finite triangles?

## Explanation and evidence

For every chosen triangle made of minimizing segments in a complete manifold with sec >= k, compare its angles and cross-distances to the triangle with the same three side lengths in the simply connected surface of curvature k. Separately hold two side lengths and the included angle fixed and compare the remaining side. The two comparison experiments have different inequality directions. Positive k requires a model size restriction. Triangle sides are chosen minimizing representatives; uniqueness in M is not assumed.

[[N-R-toponogov]] fixes the exact interface; [[N-M-toponogov]] records the additional globalization argument beyond local Rauch comparison. No claim that a Ricci bound suffices is made; see [[N-E-product]].
