# The curvature hypothesis and the line hypothesis in splitting

## Explanation and evidence

For n>=2, hyperbolic space H^n contains lines but has Ric=-(n-1)g. It cannot be a Riemannian product N×R, because every product has zero sectional curvature on planes spanning the R direction and a tangent direction to N, whereas every hyperbolic plane has curvature -1. Thus a line alone is insufficient.

A round sphere has Ric>0 and is geodesically complete. Its great circles extend for all time but cease minimizing after half a period, so they are not lines. A compact metric space cannot contain a line because a line has pairs at arbitrarily large distances. Thus a complete geodesic is not a substitute for a line.
