# What ball-volume information survives the cut locus?

## Explanation and evidence

For each complete (M^n,g) with a specified curvature bound and every base point p, compare Vol B(p,r) at all admissible radii with a space form of the same dimension. Ask separately for absolute upper bounds, monotonic relative bounds, lower bounds before injectivity fails, and equality. Does a lower Ricci bound alone ensure noncollapse? The metric may vary in counterexamples; n and the curvature bound remain fixed.

[[N-R-bishop-gromov]] and [[N-R-gunther]] have opposite hypotheses as well as opposite conclusions. [[N-M-cut-integration]] performs the cut-locus step; [[N-E-collapse]] shows why an absolute volume lower bound needs additional input.
