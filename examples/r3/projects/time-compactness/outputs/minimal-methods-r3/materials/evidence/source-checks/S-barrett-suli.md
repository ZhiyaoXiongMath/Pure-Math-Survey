Unrelated parts of the long Lemma 2 proof are not newly certified.

A nonnegative cone is only closed under nonnegative scalar multiplication. Its gauge is positive definite and degree-one homogeneous. No subtraction or triangle inequality is available. Theorem 1 allows q>1 for every p, or q=1 for finite p; the pair (infinity,1) is explicitly excluded. Compactness is in the ambient A0 space, not a claim that the limit remains in a nonclosed cone.

Lemma 1 normalizes u and v by the common positive denominator [u]+[v]. Both normalized gauge values are bounded. Subsequence limits in A0 agree because their A1 distance tends to zero and the injection identifies limits. This supports the two-point estimate directly.

The passage following (2.8) still requires justification: its index selection depends on time, while the bound (2.7) was for each fixed index before integration. This is recorded as a scoped proof concern, not a counterexample to the theorem. The r3 proof instead uses W11 amplitude, gauge truncation, compact interval integrals, Simon's criterion, and verified two-point Ehrling; it does not consume the questioned passage.

Section 4 treats the special injective homogeneous map K. The local reduction explicitly checks the inverse-lift measurability and its L1 bound, as well as matching image and derivative space E2. It does not assert that arbitrary cones possess this parametrization or that all compact maps are injective.
