Theorem 5 permits 1<=p<=infinity and has no reflexivity hypothesis. X->B is compact and B->Y a continuous injection. Bounded Lp(X) trajectories with uniform Lp(Y) translations are compact in Lp(B), with continuous representatives at infinity. The criterion is used only in its sufficient direction: compact interior interval integrals plus uniform translations. At infinity it is not a characterization of every compact L-infinity subset; a discontinuous singleton fails translations.

The proof supplies two useful explanatory choices: first apply the criterion in Y, then choose the Ehrling parameter before the Cauchy index; and cover both interval endpoints by reversing time. The r3 linear mechanism makes these choices explicit. The arbitrary-Banach W11 fundamental theorem is used in its Bochner-derivative form.
