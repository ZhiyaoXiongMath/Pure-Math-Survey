# Source selection and current reading scope

This is a bounded comparison of three established compactness interfaces, not a claim to cover the latest field. The candidate set and historical omission challenge are inherited from the preserved rc2 project. On 12 October 2026 the three controlling original PDFs were retrieved again; all raw hashes matched the historical identities.

Current retrieval queries were the exact source URLs registered in knowledge/sources. Simon's journal scan was used for Theorem 1, Theorem 5 and the supporting proof interface. Maitre's author-hosted published text was read for Theorem 2.1, Remark 2.2, Lemma 2.3 and Corollary 2.4. Barrett--Süli's arXiv v2 was read for the cone definition, Theorem 1, Lemma 1, final upgrade and Section 4 comparison.

The historical discovery identified Chen--Liu's 2012 nonlinear compactness refinement and later cone/discrete variants as neighboring candidates. Their proofs are not new supporting readings for this r3 output. They remain outside the explicit three-interface scope; the text makes no optimality or strongest-known claim for Maitre's auxiliary r>1 assumption. The omission challenge is answered locally by distinguishing failure of the displayed tail estimate from a counterexample.

Stopping reason: the selected originals and elementary reconstructed arguments determine the exact three theorems, their common interval-integral interface, the verified homogeneous overlap and both requested boundary examples. Fractional time regularity, moving domains, arbitrary parametrization of cones and PDE existence applications are not promised.
