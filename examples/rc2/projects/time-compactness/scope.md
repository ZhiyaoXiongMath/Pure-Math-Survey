# Linear and nonlinear time compactness

Fix a finite interval (0,T), T>0, and real Banach spaces. The unknown is a strongly measurable trajectory, or its nonlinear image, modulo equality almost everywhere. The target is relative compactness of a uniformly bounded family in the strong Bochner norm L^p(0,T;B), with 1<=p<=infinity. For an infinity conclusion in C([0,T];B), continuous representatives must be justified. Relative compactness means every sequence has a norm-convergent subsequence; the limit need not remain in a nonclosed cone.

Question N-question: which combinations of spatial compactness and uniform time control produce this conclusion when spatial information is (i) a compact linear embedding, (ii) a compact nonlinear map B(u), or (iii) a positively homogeneous cone gauge? In the linear route X compactly embeds in B and B continuously embeds in Y; all embeddings are fixed. In the nonlinear-map route the spaces E1,E2 and map K:E1->E2 are fixed, u varies in an L1-bounded family and time regularity is imposed on v=K(u). In the cone route M+ and its degree-one gauge are fixed, and derivatives are distributions with values in the weaker Banach space A1. Constants in bounds are independent of the trajectory. The uniform translation quantifier is: for every epsilon>0 there exists h0>0 such that for every trajectory and every 0<h<h0 the indicated norm is below epsilon.

The comparison concerns these exact interfaces, not a claim that all nonlinear compactness theorems are equivalent. A proof or a source locator is required before moving between them. There is no assumed recovery of u from convergence of K(u), no monotonicity of an arbitrary compact map, no assertion that a cone is closed under subtraction, and no L-infinity conclusion from a mere L1 derivative bound.

This is a bounded historical-mechanism survey of Simon's journal text, Maitre's published theorem and Barrett--Suli's arXiv v2. It is not an exhaustive current survey: Chen--Liu's 2012 improvement, Chen--Jungel--Liu's later cone/discrete results, fractional time regularity, moving domains and PDE existence applications are identified in discovery but their proofs are outside the selected problem. In particular r>1 is a hypothesis of Maitre's stated theorem and displayed truncation estimate, not a claimed necessary condition for nonlinear compactness.

## Reader calibration

Assumed: the reader can use completeness to turn a norm-Cauchy subsequence into a limit, use Holder and Minkowski inequalities, manipulate Bochner integrals, and apply the distributional fundamental theorem for W^{1,1} trajectories. The reader has graduate functional analysis and early doctoral PDE background, not prior familiarity with nonlinear Aubin--Lions theory.

Refreshable: compact embedding means bounded sequences have strongly convergent subsequences; the precise time-translation plus interval-integral criterion is supplied as an imported theorem; uniform approximation by totally bounded sets gives total boundedness; the W^{1,1} supremum estimate is recalculated locally.

Bridge-required: why a nonlinear map does not commute with averaging; truncation of the input and a uniform tail estimate; two-point Ehrling comparison on a nonadditive cone; the order of choosing a small Ehrling parameter and then a subsequence index; and why q=1 does not force L-infinity equicontinuity.

No originality, independent mathematical review or real-reader study is claimed. Mathematics and manuscripts are English.
