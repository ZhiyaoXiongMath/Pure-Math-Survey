The nonlinear operator (called K locally) need only carry bounded E1 sets to relatively compact E2 sets. No linearity, injectivity or monotonicity is assumed. Strong measurability and Lp membership of v=K(u) are explicit hypotheses here; compactness in this definition does not by itself imply continuity/measurability of K. U is bounded in L1(E1), V lies in Lp(E2), V is bounded in Lr(E2) for some r>1, and translations of V are uniformly small in the target Lp norm. The conclusion concerns V, not U. The printed statement also has continuous representatives for p=infinity.

Proof reading: truncate u to zero on G_M={||u||E1>M}; Markov gives |G_M|<=C/M and v_M equals v off G_M and K(0) on G_M. The r>1 bound gives a small tail proportional to |G_M|^{1-1/r}; this is the role of the exponent. The paper uses shifted Riemann sums on an interior interval, averages their L1 error over shifts s in (0,h), and finds one shift for each v. The shift is allowed to depend on v, whereas M,N are chosen uniformly. Each finite sum lies in a fixed finite Minkowski sum of the relatively compact set K({||x||<=M}); uniform approximation establishes compactness of interval integrals. Simon's Theorem 1 then supplies Lp compactness.

The manuscript will present the same truncation mechanism via the closed convex hull of a compact set: approximate that set by a finite epsilon-net, and approximate integrals by its finite-dimensional convex hull. This is an explicitly labeled local reconstruction in N-truncation, not a claim that Maitre prints that argument. It avoids both any false commutation K(int u)=int K(u) and pointwise compactness at every time. The calculation gives interval-integral compactness; Simon's characterization remains the imported final theorem.

Corollary 2.4's L1 derivative bound in E2 implies all finite-p compactness because W^{1,1} also supplies a uniform L-infinity bound. It does not give the infinity endpoint. We reproduce that elementary estimate in N-translations rather than assume ||tau_h v-v||p<=h||v'||1 for every p, which would be false without changing the exponent.

Omission challenge: original Chen--Liu 2012 Theorem 1 was located and its statement read; it replaces this r>1 assumption by L1_loc boundedness using a coupled truncation/mesh estimate. Therefore this survey never calls r>1 a necessary boundary of nonlinear compactness. It is a boundary of the selected elementary tail estimate and of this stated theorem only. No new result or originality is claimed.
