# Thematic reading design

Reader: early doctoral PDE analyst, with Bochner integration and norm inequalities assumed. Refresh the exact Simon criterion. Build nonlinear truncation, compact convex averages and two-point cone comparison before using them.

Question N-question is organized by which unknown carries spatial information. Section 1 gives full shared principals N-simon, N-maitre, N-cone and fixes uniform quantifiers. Section 2 asks how compact interval integrals are obtained: N-simon-criterion, the finite-net part of N-truncation, N-linear-mechanism, then the nonlinear tail. Section 3 asks why a cone needs two points: N-cone-ehrling, N-translations and N-cone-mechanism. Section 4 compares the exact interfaces through N-homogeneous-transfer, a common-input table, N-image-only and N-endpoint.

Integrated view follows the conjunctions and alternatives across all three mechanisms. The important synthesis is a restricted overlap, not equivalence. The r>1 tail estimate is distinguished from a true endpoint obstruction. Imported Simon criterion is stated and attributed; its full proof is not promised. The cone weak-space argument is a local reconstruction avoiding the scoped unresolved source proof passage. Every proof obligation in the plan has a corresponding displayed calculation in the manuscript.

No strongest-known theorem, independent review, originality or PDE existence result is promised. Later nonlinear translation and discrete extensions are excluded with an actual discovery challenge, not omitted through a page cap.
