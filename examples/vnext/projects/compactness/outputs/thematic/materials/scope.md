# Scope and reader baseline

The reader can use periodic derivatives, uniform convergence, the fundamental theorem of calculus, bounded real subsequence extraction and orthogonal diagonalization. The precise Holder compactness interface and admissibility mechanism are built or refreshed here.

This is a known-mathematics exposition benchmark, not a literature survey or a claim of original research. The scope is exactly the stated finite-dimensional criterion or conditional sequential closedness lemma. No arbitrary PDE existence theorem or higher regularity is claimed.
